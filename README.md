## Brad Cao

Math + CS at Columbia, working on AI safety.

I want advanced AI systems to be evaluated honestly and overseen well, with results that reduce to something checkable. Current focus:

- **Evaluations & scalable oversight** — SPAR Fall 2026 mentee on the Human-AI Complementarity project: building a leaderboard that measures how well human, AI, and hybrid judges catch unsafe or deceptive model behavior (reward hacking, monitor evasion, sycophancy). Most of the work is auditing ground truth and measuring frontier-judge headroom before a dataset ships.
- **Mechanistic interpretability** — locating the GPT-2 attention heads behind in-context learning, ablating them, and measuring what breaks against matched controls ([induction-heads](https://github.com/bradca0/induction-heads)).
- **Rigorous empirics** — double descent measured, not described, as random-feature least squares ([threshold](https://github.com/bradca0/threshold)); backtests that survive honest fill assumptions ([orderbook-sim](https://github.com/bradca0/orderbook-sim)).

Background in statistical inference and reproducible experiments. Open to collaborating on evals, interpretability, or oversight research.
